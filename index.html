THE CANDID EXPERIENCE

The Candid Experience

"Every story starts with a spark. Mine began over five years ago, built on passion, tested by hardship, and driven by a promise to rebuild stronger."

My media arts journey started at Granja-Kalinawan National High School under the Special Program in the Arts (SPA), mentored by my teacher, Sir Jonathan Dawaton Carcellar. I threw myself into every filmmaking contest possible. Later in Grades 11 and 12, under the guidance of my coach, Ma'am Janelle Ladios, I served as the Technical Director for Filipino TV Broadcasting. Balancing event photography with TVB, our team made it all the way to the Regional Schools Press Conference in Borongan, Samar.

During the 2019 pandemic, I began creating cinematic self-shot videos at home, gaining traction on Facebook. By 2022, I landed my first official client—a full 18th debut coverage for a ₱300 fee. I gave it my absolute all, proving that visual storytelling was my true calling.

However, the path wasn't always smooth. As business grew, heavy personal and family struggles overwhelmed me, leading to missed deadlines and broken contracts. I lost the trust of clients and fellow photographers, facing public disputes that damaged my name and the reputation of The Candid Chronicles.

I own my past mistakes completely. Today, I am back with clarity, maturity, and renewed dedication. I am here to rebuild the trust I lost, refine my craft, and prove through action that The Candid Chronicles delivers authentic, reliable, and high-quality storytelling.
